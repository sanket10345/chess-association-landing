03 · Ranking

Tie-breaks

Applied in this order when players finish on equal points.

  1. ABuchholz Cut 1
  2. BBuchholz
  3. CSonneborn-Berger
  4. DDirect encounter
  5. EMost wins (forfeits included)
  6. FMost games played with Black

National qualification

Top 2

The 1st and 2nd placed players get the chance to represent Himachal Pradesh at the National Amateur Championship, Assam.

Subject to the regulations of HPSCA and the national federation.

05 · Who

Eligibility & rules

  • Players must be from Himachal Pradesh, or be students currently studying in Himachal Pradesh.
  • State and district player registration is compulsory for all players.
  • HPSCA reserves the right to accept or reject any entry without assigning a reason.

Accommodation

Need help with accommodation? Get in touch with our team. Accommodation is paid for by the player.

Protests & appeals

Protests against the Chief Arbiter’s decision must be made in writing within 15 minutes, with a fee of ₹1,000, refunded if upheld. HPSCA forms a five-member appeals committee before the event.

Withdrawal

A player absent from any round without informing the Chief Arbiter will be removed from all remaining rounds.

07 · The venue

A game among the pines

Shoolini University, Solan

The campus climbs a pine-clad hillside above Solan, with forested ridges rolling away on every side and the valley opening out below. Walkways wind between the trees, the air is cool and clean, and the quiet of the hills settles over everything.

It is a calm, unhurried place to think, and to step outside between rounds. Enjoy playing the game of chess in this scenic location.
